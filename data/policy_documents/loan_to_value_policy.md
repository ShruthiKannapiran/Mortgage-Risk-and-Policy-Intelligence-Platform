# Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ratio Policy

## Overview
Loan-to-value ratio (LTV) is the loan amount divided by the appraised property value (or
purchase price, whichever is lower).

## Section 1: Maximum LTV by Loan Type
- Conventional purchase: 97% LTV for first-time buyers, 95% otherwise.
- FHA: 96.5% LTV with a minimum 580 credit score; 90% LTV for scores between 500-579.
- VA: up to 100% LTV for eligible veterans with full entitlement.
- USDA: up to 100% LTV in eligible rural areas.

## Section 2: Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI)
Conventional loans with an LTV above 80% require PMI until the LTV is reduced to 78%
through scheduled amortization, or upon borrower request once at 80%.

## Section 3: High-Risk Classification
Applications with an LTV at or above 95% are flagged as **high-risk** and require
secondary appraisal review, independent of loan type.
