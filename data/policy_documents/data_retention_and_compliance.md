# Document and Data Retention Policy

## Overview
This document summarizes retention requirements for mortgage application records and
supporting documentation.

## Section 1: Retention Periods
- Approved loan files: retained for 7 years after loan payoff or sale, per investor and
  regulatory requirements.
- Denied application files: retained for 25 months from the date of the adverse-action
  notice, per Regulation B.
- Withdrawn or incomplete applications: retained for 25 months from the date of
  withdrawal or the application deadline, whichever is earlier.

## Section 2: Data Access
Only authorized underwriting, compliance, and audit personnel may access unmasked
applicant financial data. All access is logged and reviewed quarterly.
