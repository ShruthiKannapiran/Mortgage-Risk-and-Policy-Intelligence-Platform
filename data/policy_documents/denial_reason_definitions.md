# Denial Reason Definitions

## Overview
This document defines the standard denial reason codes used across all origination
channels, aligned to Regulation B adverse-action requirements.

## Section 1: Definitions
- **Debt-to-income ratio too high**: the borrower's calculated DTI exceeds the maximum
  allowed for the loan program after underwriting review.
- **Credit history**: derogatory items, insufficient credit history, or a credit score
  below the loan program's minimum.
- **Collateral**: the appraised property value is insufficient to support the requested
  loan amount, or the property fails to meet program eligibility standards.
- **Insufficient income**: verified income is insufficient or cannot be reliably
  documented to support the requested payment.
- **Incomplete application**: required documentation was not received within the
  applicable timeframe.
- **Unverifiable information**: information provided by the applicant could not be
  verified through standard channels.
- **Other**: any reason not captured by the above, which must be documented in free text
  by the underwriter.

## Section 2: Adverse Action Notices
Every denial must generate an adverse-action notice within 30 days of a completed
application, citing the specific denial reason(s) recorded in the system.
