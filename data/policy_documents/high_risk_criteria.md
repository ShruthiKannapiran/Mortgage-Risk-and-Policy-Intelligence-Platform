# High-Risk Application Criteria

## Overview
This document defines when an application is classified as high-risk for portfolio
monitoring and secondary review, independent of the final approval decision.

## Section 1: High-Risk Triggers
An application is classified as high-risk if **any** of the following is true:
- Debt-to-income ratio is at or above 43%.
- Loan-to-value ratio is at or above 95%.
- Credit score range is below 620.
- The loan is an adjustable-rate product combined with an LTV above 90% (where
  applicable).

## Section 2: Handling
High-risk applications require a second underwriter sign-off and are included in the
monthly portfolio-risk report reviewed by the credit committee.
