# Credit Score Tier Policy

## Overview
Credit score tiers determine pricing, required down payment, and manual-underwriting
requirements.

## Section 1: Tier Definitions
- 780+: Tier 1 — best available pricing, minimal documentation overlays.
- 740-779: Tier 2 — standard pricing.
- 700-739: Tier 3 — standard pricing with a 0.125% rate add-on.
- 660-699: Tier 4 — requires manual underwriting review for conventional loans.
- 620-659: Tier 5 — FHA/VA only; conventional declined by policy.
- Below 620: Tier 6 — requires exception approval from the Chief Credit Officer; most
  loan types will be declined.

## Section 2: Manual Underwriting Triggers
Any credit score below 660 combined with a DTI above 40% requires manual underwriting
regardless of loan type or automated-underwriting-system recommendation.
